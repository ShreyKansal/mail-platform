# Themes and Accessibility

Choose an appearance and use the dashboard with a keyboard or assistive technology.

## Choose a Theme

Open the account menu at the bottom of the left navigation and choose under **Appearance**:

| Theme | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Match system** | Follows your operating system's light or dark setting and switches automatically when it changes. |
| **Light** | Always light. |
| **Dark** | Always dark. |
| **Increased contrast** | Stronger text, border and focus colours for low vision or bright environments. |

Your choice is saved in this browser only. On another browser or device, the dashboard starts with **Match system**.

## Keyboard Use

- **Tab** and **Shift+Tab** move between controls. Every focused element shows a visible focus ring.
- **Skip to content:** press **Tab** once after a page loads to reveal a link that jumps past the navigation.
- **Tables:** use **Tab** to focus links and actions within rows, and **Enter** to open a link. Wide tables scroll inside their container.
- **Dialogs:** focus moves into the dialog when it opens, stays inside it while it's open, and returns to where you were when it closes. **Esc** closes it.
- **Tabs** on detail pages, such as **Recipients**, **Timeline**, **Content** and **Details**, switch with the arrow keys.
- **Status explanations:** status labels can be focused, which shows the same explanation tooltip a mouse user sees on hover.

## Screen Readers

- Pages use landmarks (navigation, main) and one page title per page. The browser tab title also names the page.
- Form fields have visible labels, and validation errors are announced with the field they belong to.
- Statuses are never shown by colour alone. Every status has a text label and an explanation.
- Previews of your email's HTML are isolated and announced as an embedded frame.

## Reduced Motion

If your operating system asks for reduced motion, the dashboard keeps animation to a minimum.

## Reporting a Problem

If something isn't usable with your assistive technology, tell your platform administrator which page and action caused it.
