# Set Up Your Account

## Create an Account

If registration is enabled, choose **Create an account** on the sign-in page. Enter your name, work email, password and organization name. Creating an account makes you the workspace owner and opens Overview.

If public registration is closed, ask your platform administrator for access.

## Sign in and Out

Sign in with your email and password. To sign out, open your account menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Sign out**.

Repeated failed sign-in attempts temporarily lock the account. The sign-in error does not reveal whether an email address has an account.

## Review Workspace Settings

The workspace menu in the header opens **Workspace settings**, **Sending limits** and **My account**. The options you can edit depend on your role and deployment configuration.

Settings opens on **Usage**, with live project activity and organization-wide sending limits. **Team** contains the workspace name and members. **SMTP** manages sending credentials, **Integrations** links application connections, and **Account** shows your profile. **Billing** and **Documents** explain what is available for your deployment.

Sending allowances are shown under Settings, not in a sandbox banner. See [Manage sending limits](SENDING-LIMITS.md) for current behavior.

## Start Sending

1. [Verify a domain](VERIFY-DOMAIN.md) you control.
2. [Create an API key](API-KEYS.md) or SMTP credentials for your application.
3. [Send your first email](SEND-FIRST-EMAIL.md) and inspect its recipient status.
